About SoloStock Hub

A practical inventory platform for e-commerce warehouse operations.

Our purpose

SoloStock Hub is an inventory management web application designed for an e-commerce warehouse business.

The system helps users organize products, brands, quantities and inventory information. It provides a central location where product records can be added, reviewed, searched, filtered and sorted.

The project connects e-commerce with inventory, logistics and warehouse management. Its purpose is to make stock information easier to understand and manage.

Core features

  • Product and brand management
  • Database-backed inventory records
  • Product search and filtering
  • Sorting and pagination
  • Validation for product information
